# Retail -- Shipping Issues

## When to use

The customer reports a shipping delay, a package that never arrived, a delivery to the wrong address, damage during transit, or any carrier-related problem.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the problem type

- **Delayed:** Package is taking longer than expected.
- **Lost:** Tracking shows no updates for an extended period, or tracking ended without delivery.
- **Delivered but not received:** Carrier says delivered, customer says no.
- **Damaged in transit:** Package or contents arrived damaged.
- **Wrong address:** Package was sent to or delivered at the wrong location.

### 2. Gather details

Get the order number and tracking number. Pull up the tracking status from the knowledge base. Ask the customer what they are seeing on their end.

### 3. Investigate based on problem type

**Delayed shipment:**
- Check if the knowledge base shows a known delay (weather, carrier backlog, customs hold for international).
- Share whatever the tracking data shows. Do not invent reasons for the delay.
- If tracking is updating normally but slowly, explain that transit times can vary. Provide the estimated window from the knowledge base if available.
- If the delay exceeds the threshold defined in the knowledge base, offer to escalate to a carrier investigation.

**Lost package:**
- Confirm the last known tracking scan and when it occurred.
- If the package has been in transit beyond the timeframe in the knowledge base without updates, offer to file a lost package claim.
- Do not immediately promise a replacement or refund. Follow the company's lost-package process from the knowledge base.

**Delivered but not received:**
- Ask the customer to check with neighbors, building management, or any safe places the carrier might leave packages.
- Check if the tracking shows a photo or specific delivery location note.
- If the customer confirms it is genuinely missing, escalate for a carrier investigation or file a claim per the knowledge base process.

**Damaged in transit:**
- Ask for photos of the damage (packaging and item) if the company's process requires them.
- Look up the damage claim process in the knowledge base.
- Do not require the customer to return a damaged item unless the knowledge base specifically says so.

**Wrong address:**
- Check if the order has been delivered or is still in transit.
- If in transit, check whether a redirect or intercept is possible through the carrier (per knowledge base).
- If delivered to the wrong address, escalate for investigation.

### 4. Resolve or escalate

If the knowledge base provides a resolution path (reship, refund, carrier claim), follow it. If not, escalate with a summary of the investigation and what the customer expects.

## What not to do

- Do not blame the carrier without evidence from tracking data.
- Do not promise reshipment or refund before confirming the company's policy.
- Do not tell the customer to contact the carrier directly unless the knowledge base says that is the process.
- Do not invent shipping timeframes or carrier names.
