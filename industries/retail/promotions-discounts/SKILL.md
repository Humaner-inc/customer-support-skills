# Retail -- Promotions and Discounts

## When to use

A promo code fails at checkout, a gift card balance is wrong, store credit did not apply, a loyalty discount is missing, or the customer asks why a sale price changed.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the promo type

Promo code, automatic sale, gift card, store credit, referral credit, or loyalty reward. Each has different rules in the knowledge base.

### 2. Check eligibility against the knowledge base

Typical failure reasons to verify, not invent:

- Expired or not yet active
- Minimum order not met
- Excluded products or brands
- One-time use already redeemed
- New-customer-only or location-restricted
- Cannot stack with other offers

### 3. Explain the mismatch

Tell the customer which documented rule blocked the discount. Offer a valid alternative only if one exists in the knowledge base.

### 4. Gift cards and store credit

Look up balance and recent redemptions. If balance looks wrong, escalate with transaction references. Do not invent balances.

### 5. Close

If applied successfully, confirm the new order total. If not applicable, be clear and offer next steps without fabricating a manual override.

## What not to do

- Do not create or invent promo codes.
- Do not override stacking or eligibility rules without documented authorization.
- Do not promise a price match unless the knowledge base has a price-match policy.
- Do not share another customer's referral or gift card details.
