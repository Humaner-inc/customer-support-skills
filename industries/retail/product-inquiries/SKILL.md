# Retail -- Product Inquiries

## When to use

The customer asks about product availability, sizing or fit, specifications, compatibility, recommendations, or comparisons between products.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the inquiry type

- **Availability:** Is this product in stock? When will it be back?
- **Sizing / fit:** What size should I get? How does this fit?
- **Specifications:** What are the materials, dimensions, features?
- **Compatibility:** Does this work with [other product]?
- **Recommendations:** What would you suggest for [use case]?
- **Comparison:** What is the difference between [product A] and [product B]?

### 2. Look up the product in the knowledge base

Find the product by name, SKU, or description. Retrieve the available information: stock status, size chart, specifications, product description.

If the product is not found in the knowledge base, say so. Suggest checking the website directly or offer to open a ticket for the product team.

### 3. Answer based on inquiry type

**Availability:**
- Share the current stock status from the knowledge base.
- If out of stock, check if there is a restock date or waitlist option.
- Do not guess restock dates. Only share what the knowledge base provides.
- If the item is discontinued, say so clearly and suggest alternatives if available.

**Sizing / fit:**
- Share the size chart or fit guide from the knowledge base.
- If the customer provides their measurements, help them match to the size chart.
- If the knowledge base has fit notes (e.g., "runs small," "true to size"), share them.
- Do not invent sizing advice. If unsure, suggest the customer check the size chart on the product page or contact the brand.

**Specifications:**
- Share the specifications from the knowledge base (materials, dimensions, weight, features, care instructions).
- If a specification is not listed, say it is not available rather than guessing.

**Compatibility:**
- Check the knowledge base for compatibility information.
- If not documented, do not guess. Suggest the customer contact the manufacturer or check product documentation.

**Recommendations:**
- Ask clarifying questions about the customer's use case, preferences, and budget range.
- Suggest products from the knowledge base that match. Explain why each one fits.
- Do not recommend products not in the catalog.

**Comparison:**
- Pull specifications for both products from the knowledge base.
- Present the differences factually. Do not inject personal opinions or rankings.

### 4. Close or redirect

If the question is answered, confirm the customer has what they need. If they want to purchase, guide them to the appropriate page or process. If the question is beyond what the knowledge base covers, escalate to the product team.

## What not to do

- Do not invent product specifications, materials, or dimensions.
- Do not guess restock dates or availability timelines.
- Do not recommend products not in the knowledge base.
- Do not make subjective quality claims about products.
