# Retail -- Account and Orders

## When to use

The customer cannot log in, cannot find past orders, needs to update address or payment methods, merge accounts, or delete an account.

## Procedure

### 1. Verify identity before account changes

Follow the knowledge base identity checks before changing email, password, addresses, or payment methods. Never skip this for account edits.

### 2. Login and access

Guide password reset or account recovery from the knowledge base. If the email is wrong or the account cannot be found, search carefully and escalate rather than creating a duplicate account without confirmation.

### 3. Order history

Look up orders by account or email. Help the customer locate confirmation numbers. If orders are missing, check guest checkout vs account checkout and explain the difference if documented.

### 4. Profile updates

Guide or process updates to shipping addresses and saved payment methods per knowledge base. Prefer self-serve when available.

### 5. Account deletion or merge

Follow the privacy and account deletion process in the knowledge base. Confirm consequences (order history, store credit, subscriptions) before proceeding.

## What not to do

- Do not change account details without identity verification.
- Do not ask for passwords.
- Do not invent order history or claim an order exists when it does not.
- Do not merge accounts without a documented process.
