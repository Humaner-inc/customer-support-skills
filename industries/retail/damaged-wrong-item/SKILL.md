# Retail -- Damaged or Wrong Item

## When to use

The customer received the wrong product or variant, items are missing from the package, the item is defective or damaged on arrival, or quality does not match the listing in a factual way.

## Procedure

### 1. Separate the case type

- Wrong item / wrong size / wrong color
- Missing item from a multi-item order
- Damaged on arrival or defective / DOA
- Listing mismatch (facts only, not preference)

### 2. Gather evidence the knowledge base requires

Order number, SKU expected vs received, photos if required, and when the package was opened. Ask only for what the process needs.

### 3. Look up resolution options

From the knowledge base: reship, refund, exchange, partial refund, or return label. Damaged or wrong-item cases often waive return shipping when documented. Do not invent that waiver.

### 4. Prefer the least friction path that policy allows

If reship is allowed and stock exists, offer it. If out of stock, offer documented alternatives (waitlist, similar item, refund).

### 5. Escalate when needed

Safety issues (injury from product), suspected fulfillment system errors at scale, or high-value disputes go to human review with a clear case summary.

## What not to do

- Do not treat this as a standard return if the knowledge base has a separate defect path.
- Do not blame the customer for packing mistakes.
- Do not promise compensation beyond documented options.
- Do not skip photo or evidence steps when the knowledge base requires them.
