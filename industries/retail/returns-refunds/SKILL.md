# Retail -- Returns and Refunds

## When to use

The customer wants to return an item, asks about refund status, requests an exchange, or asks about the return policy.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify what the customer wants

Clarify whether they want a return, a refund, an exchange, or just information about the policy. These are different flows:

- **Return:** Customer sends the item back.
- **Refund:** Customer wants money back (may or may not involve a return).
- **Exchange:** Customer wants a different item (size, color, variant).
- **Policy question:** Customer just wants to know what is possible.

### 2. Gather the order details

Ask for the order number and the specific item(s) they want to return or exchange. Confirm the item, purchase date, and current order status from the knowledge base.

### 3. Check eligibility in the knowledge base

Look up the return/refund policy for this item. Key questions to answer from the knowledge base:

- Is the item within the return window?
- Is this item category eligible for returns (some items may be final sale)?
- What condition must the item be in?
- Are there restocking fees?
- Does the customer need to pay return shipping?

Do not guess any of these. If the policy is not in the knowledge base, say so and offer to check with the team.

### 4. Explain the process

Walk the customer through the steps:

- How to initiate the return (portal, email, label generation)
- Where to ship the item (if applicable)
- Expected timeline for refund processing
- How the refund will be issued (original payment method, store credit, etc.)

Only share details that exist in the knowledge base.

### 5. Handle common variations

- **Item arrived damaged:** Acknowledge empathetically. The customer should not have to pay return shipping for a damaged item. Look up the damage/defect policy.
- **Outside return window:** Check if there are exceptions in the knowledge base (holidays, defective items). If none, explain honestly and offer alternatives if available.
- **Refund not received:** Look up the refund status. If processed, share the timeline and suggest the customer check with their bank. If not yet processed, escalate.
- **Partial order return:** Confirm which items are being returned and calculate accordingly from the knowledge base.
- **Exchange for out-of-stock item:** Offer alternatives: waitlist, similar item, or refund.

### 6. Close or escalate

If the return/refund can be initiated, do so and confirm next steps. If the case requires manager approval, exception handling, or policy clarification, escalate with full context.

## What not to do

- Do not invent return windows, refund amounts, or restocking fees.
- Do not promise a refund timeline not stated in the knowledge base.
- Do not approve exceptions to the return policy without confirming authorization in the knowledge base.
- Do not tell the customer their item is "final sale" unless the knowledge base explicitly says so.
