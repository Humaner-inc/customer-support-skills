# Retail -- Subscription and Replenishment

## When to use

The customer wants to pause, skip, swap products, change frequency, update payment, cancel a subscription, or was charged for a renewal they did not expect.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm the subscription

Look up the active subscription: plan, next ship date, cadence, payment method, and recent charges.

### 2. Match the action

- **Skip / pause:** check allowed skip counts and pause duration in the knowledge base.
- **Swap / edit products:** check whether swaps are allowed before the cutoff for the next shipment.
- **Frequency or quantity change:** apply only documented options.
- **Payment update:** guide to secure self-serve update. Never collect full card numbers.
- **Unexpected renewal charge:** explain the renewal date and terms from the knowledge base. If cancel was requested earlier, investigate whether cancel completed.
- **Cancel:** process or guide per knowledge base. Confirm when the last shipment and last charge occur.

### 3. Cutoff awareness

Many renewals lock before ship. If past cutoff, explain that the current cycle may still ship and the change applies to the next cycle, if that is what the knowledge base says.

### 4. Confirm in plain language

Repeat next ship date, next charge date, and what the customer just changed.

## What not to do

- Do not invent skip limits, pause lengths, or cancel fees.
- Do not pressure the customer to stay when they want to cancel.
- Do not process refunds for renewals without confirming the refund policy.
- Do not ask for full card numbers or CVV.
