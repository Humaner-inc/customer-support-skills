# Retail -- Order Tracking

## When to use

The customer asks where their order is, when it will arrive, why tracking is not updating, or mentions a confirmation/order number expecting a status update.

## Procedure

### 1. Gather the order reference

Ask for the order number or confirmation number if the customer hasn't provided one. If the customer provides an email instead, look up orders associated with that email in the knowledge base.

Do not proceed without a valid reference. If the customer cannot locate their order number, suggest they check their confirmation email or account order history.

### 2. Look up the order in the knowledge base

Retrieve the order record. Confirm the order exists and share the current status with the customer. Typical statuses to expect from the knowledge base:

- Order confirmed / processing
- Shipped / in transit (with carrier and tracking number)
- Out for delivery
- Delivered
- On hold / exception

If the order is not found, tell the customer honestly. Do not guess. Offer to open a ticket for the support team to investigate.

### 3. Interpret the status for the customer

Translate the raw status into plain language. Do not copy-paste internal codes. For example:

- "Your order has shipped with [carrier]. Here's your tracking number: [number]."
- "It looks like your order is still being processed. Based on what I can see, it hasn't shipped yet."

Never invent estimated delivery dates. Only share timelines that are explicitly present in the knowledge base or tracking data.

### 4. Handle common follow-ups

- **Tracking not updating:** Explain that carriers sometimes have delays in scan updates. Suggest waiting 24-48 hours and checking again. If it has been more than the threshold in the knowledge base, offer to open an investigation.
- **Package shows delivered but not received:** Suggest checking with neighbors, building reception, or alternate delivery locations. If still not found, escalate to the support team for a carrier investigation.
- **Wrong address on order:** Check if the order has shipped. If not yet shipped, attempt to update. If already shipped, explain that address changes may not be possible and offer next steps from the knowledge base.
- **Multiple items, partial shipment:** Explain that orders may ship in multiple packages. Share tracking for each shipment if available.

### 5. Close or escalate

If the issue is resolved, confirm the customer has what they need. If the issue requires human intervention (carrier investigation, refund for non-delivery, address correction on shipped order), escalate with a clear summary of what was found.

## What not to do

- Do not invent delivery dates, carrier names, or tracking numbers.
- Do not promise reshipment or refund without confirming the company's policy in the knowledge base.
- Do not tell the customer to "just wait" without a concrete next step or timeframe from the knowledge base.
