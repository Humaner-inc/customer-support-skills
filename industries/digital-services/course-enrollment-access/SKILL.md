# Digital Services -- Course Enrollment and Access

## When to use

A student or learner paid but cannot see a course, sees the wrong course, lost access after a failed renewal, has an expired enrollment, or redeemed an access code without result.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm identity and purchase

Match the email used for login with the email on the order or license. Wrong email is a top cause of missing access.

### 2. Check enrollment state

In the knowledge base or admin tools: enrolled, preview-only, expired, revoked, pending payment sync. Compare order date with enrollment date.

### 3. Common fixes

- Attach enrollment to the correct user
- Resync after successful payment
- Extend or restore access only when the knowledge base allows
- Explain trial vs paid access differences

### 4. Access codes and institutional licenses

Follow redemption and roster rules from the knowledge base. If the school or org provides access, route the student to the right admin when required.

### 5. Escalate payment-access mismatches

Paid with no enrollment after documented sync steps escalate to billing and platform with order IDs.

## What not to do

- Do not grant free permanent access to bypass a payment bug without authorization.
- Do not invent course availability or certificate rules.
- Do not enroll the student in the wrong product to make them go away.
- Do not share another learner's progress or grades.
