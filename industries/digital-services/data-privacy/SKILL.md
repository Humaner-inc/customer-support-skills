# Digital Services -- Data Privacy

## When to use

The customer asks to export their data, delete their account, inquires about data handling practices, or references privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.).

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the request type

- **Data export:** Customer wants a copy of all their data.
- **Account deletion:** Customer wants their account and data permanently removed.
- **Data handling question:** Customer asks where data is stored, who has access, retention periods.
- **Compliance request:** Customer references a specific regulation or needs documentation for their compliance team.
- **Consent management:** Customer wants to update their communication or data processing preferences.

### 2. Verify identity

Privacy requests require identity verification before processing. Follow the verification process in the knowledge base. This is not optional, even if the customer is frustrated by the extra step.

### 3. Handle based on request type

**Data export:**
- Check the knowledge base for the self-service export option (account settings, admin panel).
- If self-service is available, guide the customer through it.
- If a manual export is required, explain the process and timeline from the knowledge base.
- Confirm what is included in the export (and what is not, if documented).

**Account deletion:**
- Explain what deletion means per the knowledge base: what is deleted, what is retained (legal holds, anonymized analytics), and the timeline.
- If there is a grace period or soft-delete window, explain it.
- Confirm the customer understands the consequences (irreversible, data loss).
- Process the request or escalate per the knowledge base process.

**Data handling question:**
- Share information from the knowledge base: data residency, encryption, access controls, retention periods.
- If the customer asks for the privacy policy or DPA (Data Processing Agreement), share the link from the knowledge base.
- Do not improvise answers about security architecture or compliance certifications. Share only what is documented.

**Compliance request:**
- If the customer asks for SOC 2 reports, DPAs, or compliance documentation, check the knowledge base for where to find these.
- If the request involves legal counsel, escalate to the legal or compliance team.

**Consent management:**
- Guide the customer to their notification or privacy settings.
- If the change requires support action, process it per the knowledge base.

### 4. Document and confirm

Privacy requests often have legal tracking requirements. Follow the documentation process in the knowledge base. Confirm the request has been logged and share the expected timeline.

## What not to do

- Do not provide legal advice or interpret regulations for the customer.
- Do not skip identity verification for privacy requests.
- Do not guess about data residency, retention periods, or compliance certifications.
- Do not delete data without following the knowledge base process.
- Do not share internal security architecture details beyond what the knowledge base authorizes.
