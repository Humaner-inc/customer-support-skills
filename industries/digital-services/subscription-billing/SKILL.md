# Digital Services -- Subscription and Billing

## When to use

The customer wants to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel their subscription, has a billing question, needs an invoice, or reports a payment failure.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the billing concern

- **Plan change:** Upgrade, downgrade, or switch billing cycle.
- **Cancellation:** Customer wants to end their subscription.
- **Invoice / receipt:** Customer needs billing documentation.
- **Unexpected charge:** Customer does not recognize a charge.
- **Payment failure:** Subscription payment was declined.
- **Seat / license management:** Adding or removing users.

### 2. Confirm the current plan

Look up the customer's current subscription in the knowledge base: plan name, billing cycle, seat count, renewal date, payment method on file. Share this with the customer to confirm you are looking at the right account.

### 3. Handle based on concern type

**Plan change (upgrade/downgrade):**
- Look up available plans and pricing in the knowledge base.
- Explain the differences between the current plan and the target plan.
- Explain how the change will be applied: immediately or at next billing cycle, prorated or not (per the knowledge base).
- If the customer is downgrading, explain any features they will lose.
- Process the change if the knowledge base allows it, or guide the customer to the self-service option.

**Cancellation:**
- Ask why the customer wants to cancel (optional, do not pressure).
- Explain what happens upon cancellation from the knowledge base: immediate loss of access vs. access until end of billing period, data retention period, etc.
- Process the cancellation or guide to self-service. Confirm the effective date.
- If there is a retention offer in the knowledge base (pause, discount), mention it once. Do not push.

**Invoice / receipt:**
- Look up invoices in the knowledge base or guide the customer to where they can download them in the product.
- If the customer needs a modified invoice (tax ID, company name), check the knowledge base for the process.

**Unexpected charge:**
- Look up the charge and match it to a subscription renewal, seat addition, overage fee, or one-time purchase.
- Explain in plain language. If the charge is incorrect, escalate for a refund.

**Payment failure:**
- Check the knowledge base for the failure reason (expired card, insufficient funds, bank block).
- Guide the customer to update their payment method.
- Explain the grace period (if any) before service interruption per the knowledge base.

**Seat / license management:**
- Look up how to add or remove seats in the knowledge base.
- Explain the billing impact (prorated charge for additions, credit for removals, or next-cycle adjustment).

### 4. Close or escalate

Confirm the change is applied and the customer understands next steps. If the action requires admin privileges or billing team access, escalate with context.

## What not to do

- Do not invent pricing, plan features, or billing terms.
- Do not pressure customers to stay when they want to cancel.
- Do not process refunds without confirming the refund policy in the knowledge base.
- Do not share other users' billing information, even within the same organization.
