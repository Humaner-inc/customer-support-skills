# Digital Services -- Permissions and Roles

## When to use

Someone cannot access a workspace or feature, needs an invite, wants a role change, removed a user by mistake, or asks who can see which data.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm who is asking

Only admins or documented owners can change roles for others. If a member requests elevated access, explain they need an admin, unless the knowledge base allows self-serve upgrades.

### 2. Diagnose access denied

Check: wrong workspace, wrong role, seat not assigned, SSO provisioning lag, or feature gated by plan. Share the specific cause from the knowledge base or account data.

### 3. Invites and removals

Guide invite flow, pending invites, and resend. For removals, confirm data ownership implications from the knowledge base before deleting access.

### 4. Role changes

Explain what each role can do using documented role definitions. Apply the change only with proper authority.

### 5. Escalate sensitive cases

Privilege escalation requests, suspected unauthorized access, or org-wide permission corruption escalate to a human with context.

## What not to do

- Do not grant admin to someone without documented authorization.
- Do not invent role capabilities.
- Do not remove the last admin without a recovery path from the knowledge base.
- Do not discuss another user's personal data beyond what the requester is allowed to see.
