# Digital Services -- Performance and Outages

## When to use

The product is slow, errors for many users, a region is affected, or the customer asks whether there is an outage.

## Procedure

### 1. Check known status first

Look up status page, incident banners, and known issues in the knowledge base. If there is an active incident, say so and share the official status link and any workaround.

### 2. Scope the impact

Ask whether it affects one user, one workspace, or many. Capture region, browser, and start time. This separates local issues from platform incidents.

### 3. Local triage when no incident

Basic checks from the knowledge base: different network, cache, browser, disable conflicting extensions, try another user in the same org.

### 4. Communicate honestly

If investigating, say what you know and do not know. Do not invent ETAs. Share the update channel from the knowledge base.

### 5. Escalate widespread impact

Multiple customers or critical workflows blocked escalate immediately as an incident per knowledge base process.

## What not to do

- Do not deny an outage when the status page shows one.
- Do not invent root cause or fix time.
- Do not tell the customer to clear cache as the only response to a platform incident.
- Do not over-promise service credits unless the knowledge base defines them.
