# Digital Services -- Plan Limits and Quotas

## When to use

The customer hit a seat limit, storage cap, message or API quota, usage overage charge, or asks what their plan includes.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm current plan and usage

Look up plan name, included limits, current usage, and billing cycle from the knowledge base or account data.

### 2. Explain the block clearly

Name the limit that was hit and the current usage vs allowance. Avoid jargon.

### 3. Offer documented options

Typical paths: free up seats or storage, wait for quota reset, upgrade plan, buy add-on packs. Only offer what exists in the knowledge base.

### 4. Overage charges

If billed for overage, explain how overage is calculated from the knowledge base. If the charge looks wrong, escalate to billing with usage evidence.

### 5. Close

Confirm whether the customer will clean up usage, upgrade, or open a billing review.

## What not to do

- Do not invent quota numbers or reset times.
- Do not silently raise limits without authorization.
- Do not shame the customer for usage.
- Do not quote upgrade pricing that is not in the knowledge base.
