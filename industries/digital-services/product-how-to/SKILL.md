# Digital Services -- Product How-To

## When to use

The customer asks how to do something in the product, where a feature lives, how a workflow works, or needs a step-by-step walkthrough. This is usage help, not a bug report.

## Procedure

### 1. Clarify the goal

Restate what they are trying to accomplish in one sentence. Confirm plan or role if feature availability depends on it.

### 2. Ground answers in the knowledge base

Use help docs, in-product guides, and changelog notes from the knowledge base. Walk through current steps. Prefer short numbered steps over long essays.

### 3. Check plan and permissions

If the feature is missing, verify whether it is on their plan or blocked by role. Do not tell them a feature exists if it is not in the knowledge base for their tier.

### 4. Distinguish how-to from bug

If steps fail when followed correctly, switch to the bug-reports skill: gather reproduce steps, environment, and escalate.

### 5. Close with a pointer

Share the relevant help article link from the knowledge base when available. Confirm the customer can complete the task.

## What not to do

- Do not invent menu paths, buttons, or features.
- Do not treat a how-to as a feature request promise.
- Do not dump the entire manual. Answer the specific goal.
- Do not claim a feature is coming on a date unless documented.
