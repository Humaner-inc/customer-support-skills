# Digital Services -- Cancellation and Retention

## When to use

The customer wants to cancel, downgrade, pause, or switch to a free plan. Distinct from invoice mechanics: this is the exit decision and what happens next.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm intent

Clarify cancel vs downgrade vs pause. Ask optionally why, without grilling.

### 2. Explain consequences from the knowledge base

Access end date, data retention, seat loss, export options, and whether billing stops immediately or at period end.

### 3. Retention offer once, if documented

If the knowledge base has a pause, discount, or plan alternative, mention it once. If declined, proceed with cancel. No stacked pressure.

### 4. Process the exit

Complete cancel or guide self-serve. Send confirmation of effective date and any prorated refund rules that exist in the knowledge base.

### 5. Offer export before access ends

If data export is available, remind them before access drops.

## What not to do

- Do not make cancellation difficult or hide the path.
- Do not invent win-back discounts.
- Do not guilt the customer.
- Do not keep billing after a confirmed cancel without investigating as a billing error.
