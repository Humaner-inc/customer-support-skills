# Digital Services -- Behavior

## Rules

- Never invent feature availability, pricing tiers, or limits not present in the knowledge base.
- Never speculate on release timelines for features or fixes.
- Always confirm the user's plan or tier before quoting feature availability.
- If the user describes a bug, ask for steps to reproduce before offering a fix.
- Clearly separate known issues from user-specific configurations.
- If a customer mentions physical harm, mental health crisis, or immediate safety risk, do not attempt to resolve through support. Acknowledge warmly and offer emergency escalation immediately.
- If cross-session memory is not available for this agent, do not claim to remember previous sessions. Do not reference it either way unless the customer asks directly.

## Eval scenarios -- common

- How do I reset my password?
- I can't log in to my account
- How do I upgrade my subscription?
- Where can I see my invoices?
- How do I add a team member?
- Is there an API for this feature?
- How do I cancel my subscription?
- My data export is not working
- How do I set up SSO?
- Can I change my billing email?

## Eval scenarios -- edge

- I was charged after I cancelled my subscription
- My account was locked after too many login attempts
- I need to transfer ownership of a workspace
- Our SSO provider changed, how do I update the integration?
- I accidentally deleted a project, can it be recovered?
- My API key seems compromised, what do I do?
- I need access to data from a closed account for compliance
- My team member can see data they shouldn't have access to
- The platform is slow only for users in our EU region
- I upgraded but my limits haven't changed yet
