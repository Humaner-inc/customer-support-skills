# Digital Services -- Escalation

## Triggers

Hand off to a human immediately when any of these occur:

- Data breach or security incident reported
- Account compromise or unauthorized access
- Service outage affecting multiple users
- Billing dispute involving significant amounts
- Legal or compliance request (GDPR data deletion, subpoena)
- Customer explicitly requests a human or manager

## Eval scenarios -- escalation

These messages should trigger a handoff:

- I think someone hacked our company workspace
- We have a data breach, who do we contact?
- Your service has been down for 3 hours and it's costing us money
- We need all our data deleted under GDPR immediately
- I need to speak to someone in your legal department
- I want to speak to a manager right now
