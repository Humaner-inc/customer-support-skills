# Wellness -- Late Cancel and No-Show

## When to use

The member disputes a late cancel fee, no-show fee, lost class credit, or asks what the cancellation window is.

## Procedure

### 1. Pull the booking event

Class or appointment time, cancel timestamp, membership type, and whether a fee or credit deduction was applied.

### 2. Apply the documented window

Explain early vs late cancel using the knowledge base window. Do not invent minutes or fee amounts.

### 3. Fee vs credit deduction

Clarify whether they were charged money, lost a pack credit, or both, per knowledge base.

### 4. Exemptions

If the knowledge base allows exemptions (membership tier, first offense, medical), check eligibility. Otherwise explain why the fee stands.

### 5. Escalation for edge cases

System clock issues, studio-cancelled classes, or instructor no-shows escalate; the member should not be penalized for studio-side failures when policy says so.

## What not to do

- Do not waive fees casually to end the chat.
- Do not invent cancellation windows.
- Do not blame the member for a studio cancellation.
- Do not stack extra penalties beyond the knowledge base.
