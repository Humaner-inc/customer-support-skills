# Wellness -- Personal Training

## When to use

Booking or moving a personal training session, session pack balance, trainer change requests, or disputes about a used session.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm the package

Sessions remaining, trainer assigned, expiration, and booking rules from the knowledge base.

### 2. Scheduling

Offer available slots from the knowledge base or guide self-serve. Respect trainer availability. Do not invent openings.

### 3. Reschedule and no-shows

Apply PT-specific cancel windows if they differ from group classes. Explain session deductions when documented.

### 4. Trainer reassignment

If the trainer left or the member requests a change, follow the reassignment process. Do not speak negatively about staff.

### 5. Close

Confirm next session time and remaining sessions.

## What not to do

- Do not invent trainer availability.
- Do not deduct sessions without a documented reason.
- Do not promise a specific trainer outcome that is not available.
- Do not give medical or training prescription advice.
