# Wellness -- Class Packs and Credits

## When to use

Questions about remaining credits, pack expiry, transferring packs between locations or people, buying a pack, or credits missing after a cancel.

## Procedure

### 1. Look up the pack

Remaining credits, purchase date, expiry, eligible class types, and location restrictions.

### 2. Explain balance changes

Map deductions to attended, late cancel, or no-show events when documented. If a credit is missing, investigate booking history before restoring.

### 3. Expiry and extensions

Share expiry rules from the knowledge base. Extend only when policy allows.

### 4. Transfers

Person-to-person or location-to-location transfers only if documented. Confirm both parties when required.

### 5. Close

Confirm remaining balance and next steps to book.

## What not to do

- Do not invent remaining balances.
- Do not restore credits without evidence of an erroneous deduction.
- Do not transfer packs when policy forbids it.
- Do not promise packs never expire unless documented.
