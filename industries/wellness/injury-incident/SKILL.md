# Wellness -- Injury and Incident

## When to use

A member reports an injury at the facility, an accident involving equipment, or a safety incident involving staff or other members.

## Procedure

### 1. Safety first

If there is an active emergency, tell them to contact emergency services and on-site staff immediately. Do not try to treat remotely.

### 2. Acknowledge and document

Capture time, location, what happened, equipment involved, and whether first aid was given on site. Stay calm and factual.

### 3. Escalate immediately

Route to facility management or the incident process in the knowledge base. This is not a normal billing ticket.

### 4. Do not diagnose

Do not suggest treatment, medication, or whether they should keep training. Suggest they follow advice from qualified medical professionals.

### 5. Follow up path

Share how the team will follow up if the knowledge base defines a timeline. Offer to note witnesses only if asked and appropriate.

## What not to do

- Do not provide medical advice.
- Do not admit legal liability.
- Do not minimize injuries.
- Do not handle this as a routine complaint without escalation.
