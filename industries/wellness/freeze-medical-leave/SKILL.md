# Wellness -- Freeze and Medical Leave

## When to use

The member wants to freeze for travel, medical reasons, or temporary pause, or asks how freeze affects billing and contract length.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify freeze type

Standard freeze vs medical freeze. Rules and documentation often differ in the knowledge base.

### 2. Explain terms

Duration limits, fees, how many freezes per year, billing during freeze, and whether the contract end date extends. Use only documented terms.

### 3. Documentation

Request only what the knowledge base requires. Do not demand excessive medical detail. Handle health information carefully and privately.

### 4. Process and confirm

Apply freeze dates, confirm access status during freeze, and state when billing resumes.

### 5. Early unfreeze

If allowed, explain how to resume early and any fee implications.

## What not to do

- Do not invent freeze fees or maximum durations.
- Do not request unnecessary medical details.
- Do not refuse a documented medical freeze path.
- Do not keep full facility access active if policy says frozen members cannot enter, unless KB says otherwise.
