# Wellness -- Membership Management

## When to use

The customer wants to sign up, cancel, freeze, transfer, upgrade, or downgrade their membership, or has questions about their current membership status.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the membership action

- **Sign-up:** New customer wants to join.
- **Cancellation:** Customer wants to end their membership.
- **Freeze / pause:** Customer wants to temporarily suspend their membership.
- **Transfer:** Customer wants to transfer membership to another person or location.
- **Plan change:** Customer wants to upgrade or downgrade.
- **Status inquiry:** Customer wants to know what plan they are on, what it includes, or when it renews.

### 2. Pull up the membership record

Look up the customer's membership in the knowledge base: plan type, start date, billing cycle, freeze history, contract terms if any.

### 3. Handle based on action type

**Sign-up:**
- Share available membership options from the knowledge base: plans, pricing, included amenities, contract vs. month-to-month.
- Explain the sign-up process (online, in-person, documentation needed).
- If there is an initiation fee or commitment period, explain it upfront.
- Do not pressure the customer into a higher-tier plan.

**Cancellation:**
- Check the knowledge base for the cancellation process: notice period, early termination fees (if under contract), effective date.
- Explain what happens after cancellation: immediate or end-of-billing-period access, data retention.
- Process or guide to the cancellation process. Do not argue with the customer's decision.

**Freeze / pause:**
- Check the knowledge base for freeze rules: maximum duration, fees, number of freezes per year, how billing is affected.
- Explain that the membership term may be extended by the freeze period.
- Process the freeze or guide to self-service.

**Transfer:**
- Check the knowledge base for transfer rules: allowed or not, transfer fees, documentation required.
- If location transfer, check if the membership type is valid at the new location.
- Process or escalate per the knowledge base.

**Plan change:**
- Show available plans and the differences from the knowledge base.
- Explain how the change is applied: immediately or at next billing cycle, prorated or not.
- If downgrading, explain any access or amenities the customer will lose.

### 4. Close or escalate

Confirm the change and share next steps (confirmation email, new billing date, etc.). If the action requires manager approval or contract review, escalate with context.

## What not to do

- Do not invent membership pricing, contract terms, or freeze limits.
- Do not pressure customers to stay or upgrade.
- Do not skip explaining early termination fees when they apply.
- Do not process cancellations without confirming the effective date with the customer.
