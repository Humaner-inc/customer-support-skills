# Wellness -- Access and Check-In

## When to use

The member cannot check in on the app, badge or QR fails, turnstile rejects them, or they are told membership is inactive at the door.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm membership status

Active, frozen, past due, or cancelled. Access failures are often billing or freeze related. Fix root status first when that is the cause.

### 2. App and QR troubleshooting

Follow knowledge base steps: update app, refresh QR, re-login, enable Bluetooth if required. Keep steps short.

### 3. Physical credentials

Lost card or fob: follow replacement process and fees if documented. Temporary access options only when KB allows.

### 4. Location restrictions

Some plans are home-club only. Explain restriction from the knowledge base rather than overriding the door system.

### 5. Escalate hardware failures

Repeated turnstile failures for many members escalate to facilities or IT.

## What not to do

- Do not instruct staff to prop doors open as a workaround.
- Do not grant access to a cancelled membership without a documented exception.
- Do not invent app bug causes.
- Do not share another member's check-in data.
