# Wellness -- Billing and Payments

## When to use

Failed membership dues, unexpected charges, invoice requests, guest-pass fees, disputed draft amounts, or updating a card on file.

## Procedure

### 1. Pull the billing record

Look up plan, draft amount, last successful payment, failed attempts, and any add-on fees from the knowledge base.

### 2. Explain the charge

Map each line item to a documented fee (dues, initiation, freeze fee, late cancel, retail). If unexplained, escalate rather than guess.

### 3. Failed payments

Share retry schedule and access impact from the knowledge base. Guide secure card update. Never collect full card numbers in chat.

### 4. Refunds and adjustments

Apply only documented refund or credit rules. Billing errors get corrected; preference-based refunds follow policy.

### 5. Confirm next draft

State the next charge date and amount after any change.

## What not to do

- Do not invent fee amounts or draft dates.
- Do not keep charging after a confirmed cancel without treating it as an error to fix.
- Do not ask for full card numbers or CVV.
- Do not waive fees without documented authorization.
