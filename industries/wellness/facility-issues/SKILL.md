# Wellness -- Facility Issues

## When to use

The customer reports broken equipment, cleanliness problems, safety concerns, lost or stolen property at the facility, temperature or noise issues, or general facility complaints.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the issue type

- **Equipment:** Broken, malfunctioning, or missing equipment.
- **Cleanliness:** Locker rooms, studios, common areas not cleaned properly.
- **Safety:** Hazardous conditions, broken fixtures, inadequate supervision.
- **Lost property:** Customer left something at the facility or something went missing.
- **Environment:** Temperature, lighting, noise, ventilation complaints.
- **Accessibility:** Accessibility barriers or missing accommodations.

### 2. Acknowledge the concern

Facility issues affect the customer's experience and sometimes their safety. Acknowledge the problem genuinely before moving to resolution.

### 3. Handle based on issue type

**Equipment:**
- Document which equipment and which location.
- Check the knowledge base for the equipment reporting process.
- If the equipment is dangerous (broken glass, exposed wires, unstable structure), flag as urgent and escalate immediately.
- If it is a minor issue (squeaky machine, display not working), log the report and share the expected response timeline from the knowledge base.

**Cleanliness:**
- Document the specific area and concern.
- Report to the facility management team per the knowledge base process.
- If the customer is currently at the facility, let them know the team has been notified and will address it.
- If it is a recurring complaint, note that in the report.

**Safety:**
- Treat all safety concerns as urgent.
- If someone is in immediate danger, advise them to contact emergency services first.
- Document the concern and escalate immediately to facility management.
- Do not downplay safety concerns or dismiss them as minor.

**Lost property:**
- Check the knowledge base for the lost-and-found process and location.
- Ask the customer to describe the item, when they were last at the facility, and where they think they left it.
- If the facility has a lost-and-found log, check it. If not, report the missing item per the knowledge base process.
- Set expectations: the facility will check and follow up within [timeline from knowledge base].

**Environment / Accessibility:**
- Document the concern and report to facility management.
- If there is an immediate fix the customer can access (move to a different studio, adjust settings), offer it.
- For accessibility issues, escalate to management. These often require facility changes.

### 4. Follow up

Share the expected response timeline from the knowledge base. If the issue was urgent (safety, hazardous equipment), confirm it was escalated and when the customer can expect a response.

## What not to do

- Do not dismiss or minimize facility complaints.
- Do not promise specific repair dates unless documented in the knowledge base.
- Do not blame the customer for lost property.
- Do not downplay safety concerns. Escalate immediately.
- Do not offer compensation (free months, credits) without authorization from the knowledge base.
