# Wellness -- Booking and Scheduling

## When to use

The customer wants to book a class or session, reschedule an existing booking, cancel a booking, join a waitlist, or asks about scheduling policies (cancellation windows, no-show fees).

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the scheduling need

- **New booking:** Customer wants to reserve a class or session.
- **Reschedule:** Customer needs to move an existing booking.
- **Cancel booking:** Customer wants to cancel a scheduled class or session.
- **Waitlist:** Class is full, customer wants to be notified if a spot opens.
- **Policy question:** Customer asks about cancellation windows, no-show fees, or booking limits.

### 2. Check the knowledge base

Look up: class schedule, trainer availability, booking rules (advance booking window, cancellation deadline, maximum bookings per week), and the customer's membership level (some classes may be restricted by plan).

### 3. Handle based on need

**New booking:**
- Check availability in the knowledge base.
- Confirm the customer's membership allows access to this class or session type.
- Book the session and confirm date, time, location, and instructor.
- Share any preparation instructions (what to bring, arrive early, waiver requirements).

**Reschedule:**
- Look up the existing booking.
- Check if rescheduling is allowed within the cancellation window from the knowledge base.
- Find alternative times and offer options.
- Confirm the new booking and cancel the old one.

**Cancel booking:**
- Check the cancellation policy from the knowledge base: deadline (e.g., 12 hours before), fees for late cancellation.
- If within the window, cancel and confirm.
- If past the deadline, explain the late cancellation fee or no-show policy.
- Do not waive fees unless the knowledge base authorizes exceptions.

**Waitlist:**
- If the class is full, add the customer to the waitlist if the knowledge base supports it.
- Explain how the waitlist works: notification method, auto-enrollment or manual confirmation, how far in advance they will be notified.

**Policy question:**
- Share the relevant policy from the knowledge base.
- Be specific: "The cancellation window is [X] hours before the class" (only if documented).

### 4. Close or escalate

Confirm the booking action and share any relevant details. If the customer's request conflicts with policy and they want an exception, escalate.

## What not to do

- Do not invent class schedules, trainer availability, or cancellation deadlines.
- Do not waive no-show fees without policy authorization.
- Do not book a customer into a class their membership does not include.
- Do not promise waitlist spots will open up.
