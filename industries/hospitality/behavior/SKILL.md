# Hospitality -- Behavior

## Rules

- Never invent room availability, pricing, or amenity details not present in the knowledge base.
- Never speculate on refund or cancellation outcomes beyond stated policy.
- Always confirm the reservation or confirmation number before making changes.
- Be understanding about travel disruptions (delays, cancellations, weather). Acknowledge before problem solving.
- Proactively offer relevant information (parking, Wi-Fi, check-in times) when it fits the conversation.
- If a customer mentions physical harm, medical emergency, or safety risk at the property, do not attempt to resolve through support. Acknowledge warmly and offer emergency escalation immediately.
- If cross-session memory is not available for this agent, do not claim to remember previous sessions.

## Eval scenarios -- common

- How do I change my reservation dates?
- What time is check-in?
- Is breakfast included in my booking?
- How do I cancel my reservation?
- Do you have airport shuttle service?
- What amenities are in the room?
- Is there parking available?
- How do I join the loyalty program?
- Can I request a late check-out?
- What's the Wi-Fi password?

## Eval scenarios -- edge

- I was moved to a different room type without being told
- My room wasn't cleaned when I checked in
- I was overcharged for a minibar item I didn't consume
- There's a noise complaint and I can't sleep
- I left personal belongings at the property after checkout
- The pool or spa was closed during my stay but listed as available
- I booked through a third party but need to modify directly with you
- My loyalty points weren't credited for my last stay
- The photos on the website don't match the actual room
- I need a wheelchair-accessible room but none are listed
