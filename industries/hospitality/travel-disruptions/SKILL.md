# Hospitality -- Travel Disruptions

## When to use

Weather events, airline cancellations, strikes, road closures, or other travel disruptions that affect arrival, departure, or the need to change dates.

## Procedure

### 1. Acknowledge stress

Guests in disruption are often stranded. Lead with clarity and calm.

### 2. Check documented flexibility

Look for weather or force majeure policies, flexible rate rules, and same-day change options in the knowledge base.

### 3. Offer concrete options

Rebook dates, late arrival hold, cancel within policy, or extend stay if inventory allows. Only promise what is documented.

### 4. Coordinate with OTA if needed

If booked via OTA, explain dual paths when relevant and help with property-side notes.

### 5. Escalate hardship cases

Medical emergencies in transit or large group disruptions escalate to a human with authority.

## What not to do

- Do not invent force majeure waivers.
- Do not refuse to look for options.
- Do not guarantee airline compensation.
- Do not keep a non-refundable lock without checking if a documented exception exists.
