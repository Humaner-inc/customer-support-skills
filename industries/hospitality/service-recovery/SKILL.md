# Hospitality -- Service Recovery

## When to use

The guest reports rude service, missed housekeeping, amenities not delivered, long waits, or a stay that fell short of what was promised.

## Procedure

### 1. Listen and acknowledge

Let the guest state the impact. Apologize for the experience without admitting legal liability beyond facts.

### 2. Fix the immediate problem

Housekeeping now, amenity delivery, room change, manager visit. Action first when possible.

### 3. Offer documented recovery

If the knowledge base allows points, F&B credit, rate adjustment, or future discount, offer within authority. Escalate if the guest asks for more than you can approve.

### 4. Log for the property

Capture department, time, and outcome so patterns can be fixed.

### 5. Confirm satisfaction check

Ask if the immediate fix helps. Schedule follow-up if the stay continues.

## What not to do

- Do not invent compensation menus.
- Do not blame individual staff by name in writing.
- Do not dismiss soft complaints.
- Do not over-compensate beyond policy to end the conversation.
