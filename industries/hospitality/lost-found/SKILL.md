# Hospitality -- Lost and Found

## When to use

The guest left something in the room, safe, or lobby, or asks the property to ship an item home.

## Procedure

### 1. Collect item details

Description, last seen location, checkout date, room number, and contact details.

### 2. Trigger the search

Follow the lost-and-found process in the knowledge base. Share realistic response timing from documentation.

### 3. If found

Confirm identity before releasing. Explain pickup vs shipping options and any shipping cost policy from the knowledge base.

### 4. If not found

Be honest. Note the report. Do not invent that it will appear. Escalate valuables per property process.

### 5. Safes and locked items

If an item may be in a room safe, ensure housekeeping or engineering follows the documented open-safe process.

## What not to do

- Do not promise the item will be found.
- Do not ship without confirming address and payment rules.
- Do not release items to the wrong person.
- Do not blame the guest.
