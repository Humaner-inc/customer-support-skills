# Hospitality -- Room Issues

## When to use

The customer reports a problem with their room: maintenance issues, noise, cleanliness, missing or broken amenities, temperature, or the room not matching what was booked.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the issue type

- **Maintenance:** Plumbing, electrical, AC/heating, broken fixtures.
- **Cleanliness:** Room not cleaned properly, stains, unpleasant odors.
- **Noise:** Noise from other guests, construction, street, or internal systems.
- **Amenities:** Missing items, broken appliances (TV, safe, minibar), wrong room type.
- **Room mismatch:** Room does not match what was booked (different view, floor, bed type).
- **Safety:** Lock not working, window damage, fire safety concern.

### 2. Acknowledge empathetically

Room issues directly affect the guest's comfort and experience. Acknowledge the problem genuinely and apologize for the inconvenience before moving to resolution.

### 3. Handle based on issue type

**Maintenance:**
- Document the specific issue and room number.
- If the customer is at the property, submit a maintenance request per the knowledge base process. Share the expected response time.
- If the issue makes the room unusable (no water, no AC in extreme heat, no electricity), offer an immediate room change if available.
- For safety issues (gas smell, electrical hazard), treat as urgent and escalate immediately.

**Cleanliness:**
- Apologize and request immediate housekeeping attention per the knowledge base process.
- If the customer is at the property, offer interim options: fresh towels and linens delivered, or a room change if available.
- If the customer has already left and is complaining post-stay, log the feedback and escalate for follow-up.

**Noise:**
- If noise is from other guests, offer to contact the front desk to address the disturbance.
- If noise is from construction or external sources, check the knowledge base for known construction schedules or ongoing issues. Offer a room change if possible.
- If the noise is ongoing and severe, escalate to management.

**Amenities:**
- If items are missing (towels, toiletries, pillows), request delivery from housekeeping.
- If appliances are broken, submit a maintenance request or offer a room change.
- If the minibar was pre-stocked incorrectly, note it to avoid incorrect charges.

**Room mismatch:**
- Compare the reservation details with what the customer received.
- If the room genuinely does not match the booking (wrong bed type, wrong view, wrong category), offer a change to the correct room type if available.
- If no matching room is available, escalate to management for a resolution (upgrade, rate adjustment).

**Safety:**
- All safety issues are escalation triggers. Do not attempt to resolve alone.
- If there is immediate danger, advise the customer to leave the room and contact the front desk or emergency services.
- Document and escalate immediately.

### 4. Follow up

If a fix was promised (maintenance, housekeeping), check back (or set up a check-back per the knowledge base process) to confirm the issue was resolved. If the customer's experience was significantly impacted, escalate for potential compensation per the knowledge base.

## What not to do

- Do not dismiss room complaints or suggest the customer is being unreasonable.
- Do not promise compensation (free night, upgrade, refund) without authorization from the knowledge base.
- Do not ignore safety issues. Escalate immediately.
- Do not blame the customer for maintenance issues.
