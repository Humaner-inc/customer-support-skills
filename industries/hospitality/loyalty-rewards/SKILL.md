# Hospitality -- Loyalty and Rewards

## When to use

The customer asks about their loyalty points balance, tier status, how to earn or redeem points, reports missing points, or has questions about program benefits.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the loyalty concern

- **Points balance:** Customer wants to know how many points they have.
- **Tier status:** Customer asks about their current tier or how to reach the next tier.
- **Earning points:** Customer asks how points are earned (stays, spending, promotions).
- **Redeeming points:** Customer wants to use points for a booking, upgrade, or other reward.
- **Missing points:** Customer did not receive points for a stay or purchase.
- **Program questions:** Customer asks about general program rules, expiration, transfer.

### 2. Pull up the loyalty account

Look up the customer's loyalty account in the knowledge base using their membership number, email, or name. Confirm the account is theirs.

### 3. Handle based on concern

**Points balance:**
- Share the current balance from the knowledge base.
- If the customer expects a different amount, check for pending points (recent stays not yet credited) or expired points.

**Tier status:**
- Share the current tier and what is needed to reach the next tier from the knowledge base.
- Explain the qualifying criteria: number of stays, nights, or points earned within the qualifying period.
- Share the benefits of the current tier and the next tier.

**Earning points:**
- Explain how points are earned per the knowledge base: per stay, per currency spent, bonus promotions, partner earning.
- If there is an active promotion, share it.
- Clarify any earning exclusions (third-party bookings, discounted rates, complimentary stays).

**Redeeming points:**
- Show available redemption options from the knowledge base: free nights, upgrades, dining, experiences, partner rewards.
- Explain the redemption process and any blackout dates or restrictions.
- If the customer wants to book a reward stay, check availability and process the redemption.

**Missing points:**
- Check the knowledge base for the stay or purchase that should have earned points.
- Common reasons for missing points: booking through an OTA (not eligible), loyalty number not attached to the reservation, points pending processing.
- If the stay is eligible and points should have been credited, submit a retroactive credit request per the knowledge base process.
- Share the expected processing timeline.

**Program questions:**
- Answer from the knowledge base: point expiration rules, point transfer options, family or household sharing, how to update account details.
- Do not invent program rules.

### 4. Close or escalate

Confirm the customer's question is answered or the action is taken. If a retroactive credit or tier adjustment is needed, escalate per the knowledge base process and share the timeline.

## What not to do

- Do not invent point values, tier thresholds, or program benefits.
- Do not credit points manually without following the knowledge base process.
- Do not promise tier upgrades or exceptions.
- Do not guess about point expiration dates. Check the knowledge base.
