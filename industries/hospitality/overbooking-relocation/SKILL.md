# Hospitality -- Overbooking and Relocation

## When to use

The guest arrives and no room is available, they were moved to another property, or they were told the reservation cannot be honored.

## Procedure

### 1. Treat as urgent service failure

Apologize clearly. Do not debate whether overbooking was intentional.

### 2. Follow the walk policy

Use the knowledge base relocation process: alternate property standard, transport, meal or night compensation rules, and who approves them.

### 3. Confirm comparable accommodation

Share where they are going, how they get there, and what costs the property covers. Get approval before promising comps beyond policy.

### 4. Document everything

Reservation number, what was offered, guest acceptance or refusal, and follow-up owner.

### 5. Escalate to duty manager

Walked guests should reach someone with authority quickly.

## What not to do

- Do not leave the guest without a next step.
- Do not invent compensation.
- Do not argue about fault at the curb.
- Do not send them to an unknown property without confirming the booking.
