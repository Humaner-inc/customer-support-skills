# Hospitality -- Check-in and Check-out

## When to use

The customer asks about check-in or check-out times, requests early check-in or late check-out, has trouble with room keys, or reports room readiness issues upon arrival.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the concern

- **Check-in time:** Customer asks when they can check in.
- **Early check-in:** Customer wants to arrive before the standard time.
- **Late check-out:** Customer wants to leave after the standard time.
- **Room not ready:** Customer arrived but the room is not available.
- **Key / access issues:** Key card not working, digital key problems.
- **Express / mobile check-in:** Customer wants to skip the front desk.

### 2. Look up the property policies

Check the knowledge base for: standard check-in and check-out times, early check-in / late check-out policies (guaranteed, subject to availability, fee-based), mobile or express check-in options, and luggage storage availability.

### 3. Handle based on concern

**Check-in time:**
- Share the standard check-in time from the knowledge base.
- If the customer is arriving early, explain options: early check-in (if available), luggage storage, access to common areas while waiting.

**Early check-in request:**
- Check the knowledge base for early check-in options: complimentary if available, guaranteed for a fee, or loyalty tier benefit.
- If it is subject to availability, explain that it cannot be guaranteed but a note will be added to the reservation.
- Process the request per the knowledge base.

**Late check-out request:**
- Check the knowledge base for late check-out options: complimentary (loyalty members), fee-based, or subject to availability.
- Explain the latest possible check-out time and any applicable fees.
- If approved, update the reservation and confirm.

**Room not ready:**
- Apologize for the inconvenience.
- Check the knowledge base for the process: estimated time until ready, option to wait in the lobby or common areas, luggage storage, complimentary amenity while waiting.
- If the delay is significant, check for alternative room options.

**Key / access issues:**
- If the customer is at the property, advise them to visit the front desk for a replacement key.
- If using a digital key, guide them through troubleshooting from the knowledge base (app update, Bluetooth, re-download key).
- If the issue persists, advise contacting the front desk directly.

**Express / mobile check-in:**
- Share the mobile check-in process from the knowledge base: app download, identity verification, room assignment, digital key.
- If the customer has trouble, walk through the steps.

### 4. Close or escalate

Confirm the request is handled and the customer knows what to expect. If the issue is at the property and needs immediate attention, connect the customer with the front desk team.

## What not to do

- Do not invent check-in/out times or fee amounts.
- Do not guarantee early check-in or late check-out if the knowledge base says it is subject to availability.
- Do not dismiss room-not-ready complaints. Offer concrete alternatives.
- Do not troubleshoot key issues beyond what the knowledge base provides. Redirect to front desk.
