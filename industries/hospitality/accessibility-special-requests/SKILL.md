# Hospitality -- Accessibility and Special Requests

## When to use

Requests for accessible rooms, allergy-aware rooms, cribs, connecting rooms, high floor, quiet room, or other special needs before or during stay.

## Procedure

### 1. Capture the need precisely

Accessibility vs preference. Medical or mobility needs get priority handling per knowledge base.

### 2. Check inventory and notes

Confirm whether the request can be guaranteed, requested, or is unavailable. Never guarantee what inventory cannot support.

### 3. Add reservation notes

Document clearly for the property team. Confirm what was requested vs what is confirmed.

### 4. Arrival day failures

If the accessible or special room is not available on arrival, escalate immediately for relocation or comparable accommodation per knowledge base.

### 5. Privacy

Do not overshare medical details. Collect only what is needed to fulfill the request.

## What not to do

- Do not guarantee a room type that is only "subject to availability" if the KB says so.
- Do not dismiss accessibility needs as preferences.
- Do not invent allergy or medical protocols.
- Do not charge surprise fees for standard accessibility accommodations unless documented.
