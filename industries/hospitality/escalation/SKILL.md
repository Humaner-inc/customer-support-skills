# Hospitality -- Escalation

## Triggers

Hand off to a human immediately when any of these occur:

- Health or safety incident at the property
- Allegation of theft at the property
- Overbooking and guest displacement
- Discrimination claim
- Legal threat
- Billing dispute involving significant charges
- Customer explicitly requests a human or manager

## Eval scenarios -- escalation

These messages should trigger a handoff:

- Something was stolen from my room
- I arrived and my room was given to someone else
- I'm having a medical emergency at your hotel
- Your staff was discriminatory toward me
- This is the third time you've messed up my reservation, I want a manager
- I'm going to leave a formal complaint and take legal action
