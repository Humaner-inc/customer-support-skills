# Hospitality -- Wi-Fi Connectivity

## When to use

The guest cannot connect, has slow Wi-Fi, needs the password, or hits a captive portal error. One of the most common in-stay complaints.

## Procedure

### 1. Share official access steps

Password, network name, and portal steps from the knowledge base. Keep it short.

### 2. Basic device triage

Forget network and reconnect, toggle airplane mode, try another device, confirm they accepted terms on the portal. Follow KB-approved steps only.

### 3. Scope the outage

One room vs whole floor vs property-wide. Property-wide issues escalate to IT or the front desk immediately.

### 4. Workarounds

If documented: wired option, business center, mobile hotspot guidance without promising speeds.

### 5. Escalate infrastructure

Repeated failures for many guests or VIP impact escalate with room numbers and timestamps.

## What not to do

- Do not invent network passwords.
- Do not promise specific Mbps speeds.
- Do not dismiss Wi-Fi complaints as unimportant.
- Do not walk guests through unsafe device settings.
