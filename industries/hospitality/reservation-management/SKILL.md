# Hospitality -- Reservation Management

## When to use

The customer wants to modify, cancel, or extend a reservation, change dates, add services, or has questions about their booking details.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the reservation action

- **Date change:** Customer wants to move their stay to different dates.
- **Room change:** Customer wants a different room type or configuration.
- **Cancellation:** Customer wants to cancel the reservation.
- **Extension:** Customer wants to extend their stay.
- **Add services:** Customer wants to add extras (parking, breakfast, airport transfer, spa).
- **Booking details:** Customer wants to confirm what is included.

### 2. Pull up the reservation

Ask for the confirmation number or the name and dates of the booking. Retrieve the reservation from the knowledge base. Confirm the details with the customer.

Check the booking source: direct, OTA (online travel agency), or corporate rate. This affects what changes can be made.

### 3. Handle based on action type

**Date change:**
- Check availability for the new dates in the knowledge base.
- Check the rate difference (if any) between the original and new dates.
- Check the modification policy: is a date change free, or are there fees?
- If booked through an OTA, the customer may need to modify through the original booking platform. Advise accordingly.

**Room change:**
- Check availability for the requested room type.
- Explain any rate difference.
- If upgrading, process per the knowledge base. If downgrading, explain any implications.

**Cancellation:**
- Check the cancellation policy from the knowledge base: free cancellation deadline, cancellation fee, non-refundable rate.
- Explain what applies to this specific booking.
- Process the cancellation and confirm whether a refund will be issued and the timeline.
- For OTA bookings, advise the customer to cancel through the booking platform.

**Extension:**
- Check availability for the additional nights.
- Quote the rate from the knowledge base (it may differ from the original booking rate).
- Process the extension and confirm the new checkout date.

**Add services:**
- Check available add-on services in the knowledge base.
- Share pricing and availability.
- Add to the reservation and confirm.

**Booking details:**
- Walk through what is included: room type, meal plan, amenities, check-in/out times, any special requests already on file.
- Clarify anything the customer is unsure about.

### 4. Close or escalate

Confirm all changes and share the updated confirmation. If the modification is not possible (no availability, OTA restrictions, policy limits), explain clearly and offer alternatives.

## What not to do

- Do not invent room rates, availability, or cancellation fees.
- Do not modify OTA bookings directly unless the knowledge base allows it.
- Do not promise upgrades or rate matches without authorization.
- Do not cancel a reservation without confirming the customer understands any fees.
