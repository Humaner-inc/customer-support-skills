# Hospitality -- Billing and Folio

## When to use

Unexpected charges on the folio, resort or destination fees, minibar disputes, deposit holds, failed payments, or requests to split a bill.

## Procedure

### 1. Pull the folio

Look up posted charges, pending authorizations, taxes, and fees from the knowledge base or PMS notes available to support.

### 2. Explain each disputed line

Map charges to documented fees (room, tax, resort fee, parking, F&B, damage). If a charge looks incorrect, investigate rather than arguing.

### 3. Minibar and incidentals

If the guest denies consumption, follow the property dispute process (review footage/logs only if policy allows, or remove when authorized).

### 4. Deposits and pre-authorizations

Explain hold vs capture using knowledge base timelines. Do not invent bank release times beyond what is documented.

### 5. Adjustments

Issue corrections only with documented authority. Confirm the updated total and how refunds appear.

## What not to do

- Do not invent resort fee amounts or tax rules.
- Do not remove charges without authorization.
- Do not blame the guest for unclear fee disclosure if the folio is wrong. Fix the folio.
- Do not ask for full card numbers.
